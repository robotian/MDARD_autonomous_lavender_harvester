# Bill of materials: j100_0921

MTU's Clearpath Jackal J100, serial `j100-0921`. Derived from `robot_data/j100_0921/robot.yaml`
(Clearpath config), `mtu32_description/urdf/robot_description_j100.urdf.xacro` (custom URDF) and the
drivers `mtu32_bringup/launch/bringup_main.launch.py` starts. Qty is per robot. Items whose exact
part number is not in the config are marked *(model not in config)*.

Priced version with an online price source per row: `docs/bom_j100_0921.csv` (prices re-checked online 2026-11-11: the IMU in USD, the wireless charging kit priced from a EUR listing). `docs/bom_j100_0921.xlsx` is the 2026-11-06 lookup and has not been updated.
